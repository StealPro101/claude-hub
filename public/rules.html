RULES

▸ short and plain ◂

PUTTING A GAME ON THE HUB

  • Only games in a public repo on your own GitHub account. The Hub plays it from your own address and never from a link you type.
  • No forks and no copies of someone else’s work. Your own game, or something you are allowed to use — and keep its licence notice.
  • No keys, tokens, passwords or personal information in a game, ever. Nothing in there that is not meant to be public.
  • Nothing hateful, nothing sexual, and nothing made to harm, scare or trick whoever plays it.
  • Never ask players for a login, an account or money.
  • Up to 20 games per person.

THE BORING PART

  • The Hub owner can remove any game or any member, at any time, without a reason.
  • GitHub’s own terms apply to your account and to anything you put on it.
  • Signing in tells the Hub who you are (name, picture, bio) and nothing else. No scopes are asked for, nothing can be written to your account, and your GitHub token is never kept.
  • Games run on their creator’s own page, in a sandbox. The Hub does not check anyone’s code — play at your own risk.

SOMETHING WRONG WITH A GAME?

Tell the Hub owner directly — say which game and what is wrong. He can hide it in seconds. If it is dangerous or against GitHub’s terms, report the repo on GitHub as well.
